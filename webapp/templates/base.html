UOA Screener

Institutional & unusual options flow, scored across independent axes
A discretionary screening terminal: it surfaces unusual options activity and breaks each print down across flow, convexity, dealer gamma and catalyst timing — so you read it and decide. The combined score ranks how unusual the flow is; it is not a standalone buy signal, and the system never auto-trades. (In backtests the raw score alone showed no mechanical edge — which is precisely why the full per-axis context lives here, for a trader’s judgment.)
