Each row is a record the owner typed: its side, its price and the slippage against the card's own quote. A single record is not a statistic and is never presented as one; - the summary. Below `fills.min_n_for_stats` it shows a COUNT and nothing else ("4 dolum kaydı; istatistik için yetersiz örnek"); at or above it, the median and the interquartile range of the sample named by `summary_scope`. There is no hit rate, no average, no t-statistic and no significance claim at any sample size. The summary carries NO card-level quote. Its sample spans every card in `summary_scope`, so printing this one card's spread and bid/ask inside the same box would read as a median OF THAT QUOTE. The card's own quote belongs to the form (_alfa_fill_form.html), where it is the assumption the owner is about to test, and to each fill row, where it is the assumption that fill was measured against. `slippage_stats` returns None for every statistic below the gate, so this template cannot show one even if it tried.
