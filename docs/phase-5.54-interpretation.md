# What the interval study can establish

The live observation cadence, a feature's candle duration, entry latency and the
holding endpoint are four different choices. A fast poll does not provide a
tested fast-chart strategy. Daily ATR14 and prior-close confirmation remain in
the current live plan; the new candle gates only condition the original frozen
signals. They do not regenerate entries, estimate VWAP or evaluate an EMA rule.

The primary estimand is the mean daily **matched-SPY excess dollar difference**
between each endpoint and the existing 60-minute reference, on one prespecified
calendar-feasible panel. It uses stock whole-share reference units and an SPY
return applied to the same stock debit, not a separately rounded SPY order.
The same dates and original signal identities are retained. All other costs,
delays, rosters and candle gates are descriptive sensitivity cells. Showing a
positive conditional subset is not an incremental causal gate effect.

A longer endpoint changes both exposure duration and overlap. A larger total
therefore cannot by itself identify a healthier strategy. The ledger records
continuous-path gross underlying MFE/MAE where available; those are diagnostics,
not a guaranteed stop/target execution, maximum account loss, risk-adjusted
alpha or portfolio drawdown. A capital-constrained portfolio and defensible
quote-based execution evidence would be separate studies. Overnight corporate
actions, dividends and source price adjustments are not independently verified.

The bootstrap bands are descriptive resamples of this already inspected sample.
Shared five-session blocks do not guarantee that all dependence from overlapping
multi-session trades is captured. Neither the bands nor the largest tested
outcome authorize declaring an optimum or promoting a live rule. The old sample
is not made out-of-sample by splitting it again. All tried cells, missing labels,
early unknown candle gates, per-side costs and repeated original controls remain
in the published record. The reserved Study G and post-cutoff labels stay sealed.

Headline presence is not company valuation or favorable news. Physically captured
news is now collected separately for future fixed-arm evaluation. Historical
news causality, point-in-time fundamentals, earnings-surprise analysis, option
returns, broad-universe capacity and calibrated action probabilities are not
established by the horizon study. A future stronger candidate needs its own
fixed proposal and genuinely subsequent observations, with costs, timestamp
availability and the same coverage discipline.
