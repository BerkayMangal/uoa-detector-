# PAPER journal navigation

The PAPER journal is reachable from the main navigation and the prominent
button on Results. Historical Results keeps its transaction list before the
research disclosures. The original tracking table presents share count, time,
stop, and target with separate labels.

The journal opens with candidate count, measured count, and net at ten basis
points per side. The next section is the filterable transaction list. A record
opens to the fixed-hold prices and timestamps. The original risk arms,
other cost levels, and individual scan evidence remain available as details.

The authenticated production Results view was read before the change. The
production journal route returned HTTP 404. The updated application routes
were checked in Chromium at 1440, 390, and 320 pixels with a disposable fixture.
This covered navigation, empty state, a priced record, cost disclosures, and
the stock and SPY prices at mobile widths. The preview uses synthetic data.
The shared navigation test preserves all sixteen prior destinations and
asserts the additional visible PAPER destination.
