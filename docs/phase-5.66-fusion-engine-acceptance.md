## Real semantic inference

Use the owner's existing Mahalle/Grok infrastructure through a new dedicated
private structured endpoint, with a separate service token. No public endpoint,
arbitrary prompt/URL proxy, web tools or credential sharing. A packet contains
at most3 recent source-bound headlines for one ticker, input<=16384bytes and
headline<=1600characters; preserve actual limited coverage. System instructions
cannot come from article text. Schema-enforced model output must distinguish
issuer linkage, event type, direction, mixed/negated/speculative/confirmed facts,
materiality, uncertainty, reasons and exact supporting source spans. Reject
invented spans/IDs/tickers/numbers, malformed/future or contradictory records.
Provider sentiment and cached Mahalle annotations remain separate inputs.

Model/prompt/schema/version/input hashes, physical request/receipt/inference
completion and post-commit availability are retained. Model confidence is about
interpretation, never a trading success probability. Sentiment is not earnings
surprise or a proven forecast. Earnings/guidance comparisons require matching
period/currency/accounting basis. Price incorporation requires observed price
evidence, never text-only assertion. Missing article body/consensus means unknown.

The Mahalle service uses its existing configured Grok model and private xAI
client with tools disabled, store=false, output<=2048tokens, timeout<=45s and
zero retry. Globally at most8 new inference attempts/day across restarts, one
durable owner before billing, immutable cached outcomes keyed by input+model+
prompt/schema. Unknown or incomplete attempts never replay. Authentication or
unknown provider outcome halts new attempts for that day. Report actual usage
when available; a token/request bound is not a guaranteed dollar charge.

UOA reads existing physically recorded UW and curated sources; no new headline
vendor call. At most4 newly observed cohort tickers/day trigger inference, one
bounded frozen packet per candidate; old stories interpreted today acquire today's
availability. No backdated historical semantic label. GET performs only bounded
reads, no inference, collection, fitting or DDL. Keep raw input/output private.

## Research engine and signal criteria

Typed event rows carry ticker/session, event/first-known clocks and per-feature
provenance/missingness; labels carry their actual maturity/receipt clocks and
instrument/method. Candidate evaluation requires known inputs before its entry.
News/event direction, flow acceptance/dominance, relative price/volume/ATR,
market context and fundamental/event flags are separately selectable components.
Same-roster component removals distinguish contribution from selection changes.

A new profile freezes the exact grid before first return calculation, bounded
at8192 strategy configurations. Initial technical/direction grid targets5184
cells:6families×4lookbacks×4thresholds×3volume gates×3regimes×2directions×3daily
horizons; families may have explicitly inapplicable cells, never duplicate them
as independent trials. Intraday15/60-minute labels and daily1/3/5-session labels
are separate panels. Every model/ticker/direction/horizon/cost/variant trial and
blocked/incomplete configuration is counted; do not claim billions were run.

Use whole-date expanding splits shared across names, training-only selection,
purge at least maximum holding horizon plus an embargo, costs/delay controls,
concentration/best-trade-removal diagnostics and joint resampling accounting for
the entire searched family. Historical reused validation remains exploratory.
Ticker estimates shrink toward global estimates; fewer than60 training and20
validation resolved observations spanning at least20/10 distinct dates blocks an
independent ticker model. Missing modalities cannot become neutral zeros.

Minimum80 genuinely eligible prospective sessions and complete selected labels
are required before claiming inferential readiness or promoting a fitted rule.
Old80-session experiments are unchanged. News-semantics/fundamentals weights and
criteria are not chosen from the four observed outcomes. No automatic promotion
from the best historical configuration. A negative or inconclusive result stays.

Initial engine states are PAPER_ACCEPT/PAPER_SKIP/PAPER_UNKNOWN with explicit
reasons/coverage, not new unvalidated AL/SAT advice. Reuse existing paired stock
references only when the new decision was physically confirmed at least5s before
their entry target; otherwise that arm is unknown, never retrospectively scored.
Preserve all old targets/receipts. Short-stock mirrors remain diagnostic without
borrow/locate evidence; stock returns do not supply CALL/PUT premium returns.
